    Practical user guide

    From your first workflow to a customer launch

    Relay helps your team define a repeatable onboarding process, reuse it for each customer, and keep the work and handoffs visible. Follow the steps in order, or jump to the section you need.

    Step 1

    Map your team's process

    Open Process maps and create a map with a name and purpose. Add the work in the order your team performs it. For each step, provide a clear name, instructions, responsible role, assigned teammate, stage, and due-after-start timing. Save each step.

    For example: kickoff, customer setup, validation, and launch. Add prerequisites when one task must wait for another. Use the process check to catch missing instructions or ownership before you reuse the process.

    Step 2

    Save a reusable template

    After the process check passes, choose Create template. A template is a reusable snapshot of that checked process. Use a name your team can recognize, such as "Standard SaaS onboarding".

    If your approach changes later, update and check the process, then save a new template when you want a new starting point. Existing customer implementations are not rewritten when a template changes.

    Step 3

    Start a customer's implementation

    Open Implementations, choose a saved template, enter the customer company and contact details, and optionally set a target launch date. Relay creates a customer-specific work plan from the template.

    Open the customer record to review the plan, assign work, keep task status and dates current, and maintain the customer launch plan. Invite named contacts to the customer portal when they need access.

    Step 4

    Request customer information safely

    In Customer information, create a specific request, explain why it is needed, choose the expected answer type, and apply it to every implementation or a particular template. Customers can respond through their private implementation link.

    Only request information needed for onboarding. Never ask customers to submit passwords, payment-card details, or other secrets. Use the implementation's customer files area for supported project files.

    Step 5

    Keep delivery details together

    Inside an implementation, use Delivery operations to record SOW scope, milestones, exclusions, and stage gate; budget, forecast, actual cost, and invoice status; time entries; approval requests; and timeline events.

    Use Launch readiness and handoff to document success criteria, testing, training, go-live status, stakeholders, and risks. These are tracking tools: Relay does not generate invoices, collect payments, or automatically configure a customer's software.

    Step 6

    Review progress and workload

    Use Overview for a quick view of customer work and follow-ups. Open Team workload to review assigned open tasks and implementation health. In Implementations, search and filter by status, owner, or attention needed; export the portfolio summary when you need a CSV.

    Estimated finish dates are directional indicators based on completed work, not delivery commitments. Keep task ownership and status up to date for the most useful view.

    Step 7

    Configure your workspace

    Workspace owners can invite teammates in Settings and manage available connections in Integrations. If HubSpot is configured for your workspace, an owner can connect it and select the Closed Won pipeline, stage, and Relay template for onboarding handoff.

    Email reminders and customer invitations require the email service to be configured for your workspace. Check the connection status in Relay; sample-workspace changes do not contact HubSpot or send email.

    Any time

    Ask Ray when you need a pointer

    Open Ray from the button in the lower corner and ask a question such as "How do I start an implementation?" or "What is a process map for?" Ray provides built-in Relay product guidance; it does not inspect your workspace or make changes for you.

    For account-specific issues, contact Relay support. Never paste passwords, access tokens, or customer secrets into Ray or a customer-information request.

    Using the Sample Workspace?

    It contains fictional example data. Changes are saved only in this browser and are not connected to a Relay account, database, HubSpot, or email delivery. Use your signed-in workspace for real customer work.
