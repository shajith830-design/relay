A quick look at the name

The relay behind the Relay name.

A small interactive side note on the electrical component that inspired the brand. The product itself is onboarding software.

COMPONENT LIBRARYFACTS, NOT A WIRING DIAGRAM

Relay selected · a coil moves contacts between circuits.

⌁ A metaphor can carry the current. It cannot carry a voltage.

COMPONENT 01 · ELECTROMECHANICAL

Relay

The electrical fact

Current through a coil creates a magnetic field that moves an armature and changes contacts. A relay can use one circuit to switch another; designs differ.

The software inspiration

Relay gives a handoff a visible next step and an owner. The app passes no current and moves no electrical contact.

Bench rule: This is a software-brand analogy, not an electrical design, safety check, or wiring instruction.

01Who owns the next step?Tasks move between people without a clear handoff.+

In Relay · Task ownership

Put an owner and next action on the task.

02Where is the setup information?Customer details are scattered across messages and forms.+

In Relay · Customer information

Make the request clear and track the response.

Create an information request with an explanation and expected answer type, then share it through the customer’s private implementation portal. Relay validates supported response formats; your team still reviews what the customer submits.

Request only what onboarding needs. Never ask customers to submit passwords, payment card details, or other secrets.

03Are we ready to launch?The status is hard to explain, or key checks have no recorded owner.+

In Relay · Launch readiness

Keep the checks and risks visible.

Record success criteria, testing and training status, go-live readiness, stakeholders, and risks alongside the implementation tasks. Relay helps your team review the information; it does not configure a customer’s software or certify that a launch is safe.
