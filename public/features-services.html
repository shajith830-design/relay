Frequently asked questions

About Relay customer onboarding software.

What is Relay?

Relay is a B2B SaaS customer onboarding workspace. It helps teams map and reuse onboarding processes, coordinate customer information and tasks, and review launch readiness and portfolio progress.

Who is Relay for?

Relay is for implementation, onboarding, and professional-services teams at B2B SaaS companies that guide business customers through multi-step setup and launch work.

Does Relay configure a customer's software?

No. Relay organizes the onboarding workflow, responsibilities, customer inputs, and readiness information. Your team still performs and validates the customer's product configuration.

Which integrations and reminders are available?

Workspace owners can configure an optional HubSpot handoff for a selected pipeline and Closed Won stage. Email reminders are configurable when Relay's email delivery and scheduled processing are set up.

Does Relay generate invoices or process payments?

No. Relay can track budget, forecast, actual cost, margin notes, invoice status, and an invoice reference. It does not generate invoices, accept payments, or connect to accounting systems.
