What’s holding up your next handoff?

Choose the snag that feels familiar. We’ll show the Relay workflow that relates to it.

01“Who owns the next step?”Tasks are moving between people without a clear handoff.+

In Relay · Task ownership

Put an owner and next action on the task.

Build a process with task owners, due dates, customer or team responsibility, and prerequisites. In an implementation, teammates can update task status and see what is waiting.

A task assigned to “someone” is still waiting for its name tag.

02“We’re still waiting on information.”Customer details are scattered across messages and forms.+

In Relay · Customer information

Make the request clear and track the response.

Create an information request with an explanation and expected answer type, then share it through the customer’s private implementation portal. Relay validates supported response formats; your team still reviews what the customer submits.

Request only what onboarding needs. Never ask customers to submit passwords, payment card details, or other secrets.

See the fictional sample workspace →
03“Are we actually ready to launch?”The status is hard to explain, or key checks have no recorded owner.+

In Relay · Launch readiness

Keep the checks and risks visible.

Record success criteria, testing and training status, go-live readiness, stakeholders, and risks alongside the implementation tasks. Relay helps your team review the information; it does not configure a customer’s software or certify that a launch is safe.

The wider workflow

One place for the work behind every launch.

01

Build a repeatable process

Map onboarding steps, assign owners, and reuse templates for future customers.

02

Coordinate team and customer tasks

Track responsibilities, due dates, prerequisites, and customer information requests in one plan.

03

Give customers a clear portal

Invite named customer contacts to a private portal to respond to requests, complete shared tasks, and ask questions. Links can be revoked and expire.

04

See launch readiness

Review portfolio health, progress, overdue work, and blockers; export reports to CSV.

05

Manage delivery

Track scope, approvals, stage gates, and important timeline events.

06

Connect your workflow

On eligible plans, configured HubSpot handoffs can start an implementation from a matching deal. Configured Slack alerts and email reminders can share team updates.
