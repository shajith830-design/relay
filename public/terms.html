Service terms

Using Relay.

These service terms should be read with the order or service agreement that identifies the service provider, subscription, and applicable commercial terms.

Accounts and workspaces

Keep access under your control.

  • Provide accurate account details and protect your sign-in credentials.
  • You are responsible for access granted to members of your workspace and for information entered by your team.
  • Use customer setup links only for the intended implementation and share them with the intended participants.
  • Do not use Relay to request passwords, payment card numbers, or other authentication secrets from customers.

Acceptable use

Use Relay lawfully and responsibly.

Do not interfere with the service, attempt to access another workspace, misuse another person’s data, or use Relay in violation of applicable law. Contact the service operator through the Contact page if you believe an account or customer link has been compromised.

Service boundaries

Check product fit and terms.

Relay provides the workflows described on the product pages. The service does not synchronize with external project tools. No availability commitment or response-time guarantee is stated here; any such commitment must be agreed in writing.
