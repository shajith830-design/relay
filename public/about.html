Why this problem matters

Implementation depends on more than a task list.

A B2B SaaS customer may need to provide information, make decisions, and complete product setup before implementation can proceed. Teams need a repeatable process and a clear view of incomplete work. Relay keeps process steps, implementation tasks, and requested customer information together in its own workspace.

What Relay does

Connect the plan to customer work.

  • Map ordered onboarding steps, owners, due dates, and prerequisites.
  • Check that steps have instructions and owners before handover.
  • Save tested process snapshots as reusable templates; Growth and Custom add revision history and restore.
  • Give customers a private portal for requested information, delegated tasks, questions, and files.
  • Review basic portfolio health and export implementation data as CSV; Growth and Custom add the full workload view.
  • Configure email notifications and due-date reminders when email delivery is set up.
  • Track SOW scope, stage gates, approvals, and timeline events on all plans. Custom adds time tracking, budgets, burn alerts, and margin reporting.
  • Growth and Custom can start implementations from configured HubSpot Closed Won deals and sync a separate onboarding deal.

Who Relay is for

B2B SaaS customer onboarding teams.

Relay serves SaaS companies whose business customers go through a multi-step onboarding journey—not individual users of business software.

One focused workspace

A standalone customer onboarding workspace.

Relay brings onboarding process maps, customer information requests, and customer tasks together in its own workspace. Teams use Relay to manage the workflows and tasks created inside Relay.

Clear product boundaries

Readiness signals you can inspect.

  • See missing or invalid required customer responses.
  • Review overdue tasks and prerequisites; Growth and Custom include explicit blocker tracking.
  • Keep task status and responsible roles visible in each implementation.
  • Share only designated tasks and requested setup information through the customer portal.
  • Starter includes basic portfolio health and CSV export; Growth and Custom add team workload.
  • Send configurable email reminders for upcoming and overdue tasks after email setup.
  • Growth and Custom HubSpot can start matching Closed Won onboarding and receive a separate Relay onboarding summary.
  • Custom financial rates, time, budgets, and margin reports are internal-only and are not shown to customers.
