A SMALL BRAND STORY

Why Relay? A name built around a handoff.

An electrical relay uses a small signal to change a connection elsewhere. That simple handoff inspired our name: Relay helps teams pass customer work along with clearer owners and next steps.

Real relays vary in design and contact arrangement. This illustration is a learning aid, not wiring guidance.

Who we are

Built around a practical idea.

Relay Automation was founded by Mohamed Shajith, an entrepreneur interested in technology and practical business challenges. Relay focuses that interest on the coordination behind B2B SaaS customer onboarding.
