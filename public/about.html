About Relay

Help implementation teams see what each customer needs next.

Relay gives B2B SaaS teams one workspace to map onboarding steps, reuse a tested process, request customer information, and track implementation tasks.

Why this problem matters

Implementation depends on more than a task list.

A B2B SaaS customer may need to provide information, make decisions, and complete product setup before implementation can proceed. Teams need a repeatable process and a clear view of incomplete work. Relay keeps process steps, implementation tasks, and requested customer information together in its own workspace.

What Relay does

Connect the plan to customer work.

  • Map ordered onboarding steps and their owners.
  • Check that steps have instructions and owners before handover.
  • Save tested process snapshots as reusable templates.
  • Track customer tasks and missing required information.

Who Relay is for

B2B SaaS implementation teams.

Relay serves SaaS companies whose business customers go through a multi-step implementation—not individual users of business software.

01

The buyer

A B2B SaaS company seeking clearer, more reliable implementation readiness for its business customers.

02

The daily team

Implementation, onboarding, or professional-services staff who coordinate customer inputs and product setup.

03

The participants

The SaaS company’s customers provide agreed setup information and work with the implementation team through the journey.
