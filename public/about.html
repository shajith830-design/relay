⚡Our Philosophy & Mission

Built specifically for independent trades, not bloated tech conglomerates.

Relay Automation was founded to solve a frustrating reality: independent HVAC contractors shouldn't have to navigate complex software dashboards, hire expensive marketing agencies, or manage confusing workflows just to answer their missed calls.

The reason we’re here

Keep the focus on the work.

The Field Service Reality

Field work shouldn’t mean lost leads.

Independent 2-to-10 truck HVAC operators live in the field, under houses, and on rooftops. When office lines ring out while everyone is deployed on service calls, high-paying system replacement and emergency repair leads vanish to local competitors. Most existing software solutions either leave texts sitting unread in a chaotic inbox or require enterprise software retainers that drain small business margins.

Our Managed Approach

We handle the moving parts.

We take care of everything end-to-end as a done-for-you service. From navigating US carrier A2P 10DLC registration to tuning custom business-hour scripts, we act as your silent operational utility. You focus on fixing equipment and running your crew; we help make sure missed calls are captured and handed off. Registration approval and message delivery remain subject to carrier policies.
