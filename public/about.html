The component behind the name

A relay switches one circuit with a signal from another.

A control current energizes the coil. Its magnetic field moves an armature, which changes the contacts in a separate load circuit. This simplified example shows a normally-open contact.

Real relays vary in design and contact arrangement. This illustration is a learning aid, not wiring guidance.

Who we are
