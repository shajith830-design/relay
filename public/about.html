⌁ WHY THE NAME RELAY?

A small electrical idea. A clearer path for onboarding.

An electromechanical relay uses a signal in one circuit to switch contacts in another. Relay borrows that handoff idea for B2B SaaS onboarding: make the owner, next step, and open connection visible.

CONTROL SIDE → CONTACT SIDE

A coil moves a contact. A signal crosses a gap.

Current through the control coil creates a magnetic field. The field moves an armature, changing contacts in a separate load circuit. This simplified illustration shows a normally-open contact.

Real relays vary in design and contact arrangement. This illustration is a learning aid, not wiring guidance.

Who we are
