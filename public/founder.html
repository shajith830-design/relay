Mohamed Shajith, Founder of Relay Automation

Mohamed Shajith founded Relay Automation and built Relay, its B2B SaaS customer onboarding workspace for implementation and onboarding teams.

LinkedIn

Why Relay was built

Make the handoffs from contract to go-live visible.

Customer onboarding can stall when setup details, task ownership, prerequisites, and follow-ups are scattered across emails and spreadsheets. Relay brings reusable workflows, customer responses, delivery tracking, and launch readiness into one workspace.

About the product

Relay is made for B2B SaaS customer onboarding.

Teams use Relay to map onboarding processes, reuse templates, coordinate customer information and tasks, and review progress. Relay organizes the work; it does not automatically configure a customer's software.
