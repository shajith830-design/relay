About Shajith

Building technology that addresses real-world challenges.

I’m an entrepreneur passionate about technology, business innovation, and building solutions that address real-world challenges.

As the founder of Relay Automation, I’m working towards building technology that makes business operations simpler, more organized, and efficient. My interests extend beyond software development into entrepreneurship, business strategy, product innovation, and understanding how technology-driven businesses grow.

Relay Automation is a SaaS venture focused on improving how B2B businesses manage customer onboarding and implementation workflows. Its mission is to reduce operational complexity, improve coordination, and help businesses deliver better customer experiences.

I believe in learning through execution, taking ownership of ideas, and building from the ground up. I value persistence, discipline, continuous learning, and the willingness to move forward despite setbacks.

My ambition is not limited to building a single product, but to grow as an entrepreneur, explore meaningful opportunities, and create technology-driven businesses that deliver lasting value.

Personal Philosophy
