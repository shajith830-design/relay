Common questions

Before you get started.

Clear answers about fitting Relay into your current setup and handling urgent messages.

Do I need to switch my current phone provider or field service CRM?+

Not at all. Relay is designed to work alongside existing phone setups—including RingCentral, Ooma, Verizon, and compatible VoIP lines—and independently of CRMs like Housecall Pro or Jobber. Integration depends on your current call routing and provider features.

Is this messaging system fully compliant with US carrier regulations?+

We support A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration and configured opt-out handling. Carrier registration is subject to review and approval; no service can guarantee messages are never flagged or filtered, and delivery depends on carrier policies and availability.

What happens if a customer texts about a dangerous emergency like a gas leak or carbon monoxide?+

A specifically configured safety workflow may detect selected keywords, instruct the caller to move to safety and dial 911, and alert your on-call manager. Keyword detection, delivery, and response are not guaranteed. This is not emergency dispatch; anyone in immediate danger should call 911 directly.

How long does setup take once we sign up?+

Configuration and testing can often be completed within 24 to 48 hours, depending on the complexity of your phone setup and carrier A2P registration approval times. Timelines are estimates, not a guarantee.

Can we customize the text messages and business hours?+

Yes. During setup, we work with you to tailor the greeting, qualifying questions, and operating hours so the workflow follows your preferences and configured call rules.

Are there any hidden usage fees or long-term contracts?+

The listed plan is $150 setup plus $99 per month. No long-term commitment is represented here. Carrier, messaging-volume, and provider terms can vary; confirm applicable limits and cancellation terms before starting.
