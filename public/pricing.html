Before you get started

Pricing questions.

Know the plan limits and terms before purchase.

Are taxes included?

No. Starter and Growth prices are before applicable taxes. Relay will confirm applicable taxes and your total before payment.

What counts as an active implementation?

An implementation is active while it has unfinished tasks or required customer information that has not been submitted. Completed implementations do not use an active slot.

Can I change plans?

Contact Relay to change a workspace plan. New plan limits apply after the change; existing workspace data is retained.

Does Relay connect to external project tools?

No. Relay is a standalone workspace and does not synchronize with external project or communication tools.
