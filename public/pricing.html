Before you get started

Pricing questions.

Know the plan limits and terms before purchase.

Are taxes included?

No. Starter and Growth prices are before applicable taxes. Relay will confirm applicable taxes and your total before payment.

What counts as an active implementation?

An implementation is active while it has unfinished tasks or required customer information that has not been submitted. Completed implementations do not use an active slot.

Can I change plans?

Contact Relay to change a workspace plan. New plan limits apply after the change; existing workspace data is retained.

What does the HubSpot integration do?

A workspace owner can select a HubSpot pipeline, its Closed Won stage, and a Relay template. Newly matching deals start one implementation; unrelated deals are not imported, and the original sales deal is not changed. Relay also sends progress counts to a separate onboarding deal. Provider setup is required.

How do customer portal links and files work?

Each invited customer contact gets an individual link that expires after 30 days and can be revoked by your team. Customers can answer requests, complete selected tasks, send messages, and upload PDF, PNG, or JPG files up to 5 MB each, with a limit of 20 files and 25 MB per implementation. Do not upload passwords, payment details, or other secrets.

Does Relay generate invoices or process payments?

No. Relay can track budget, forecast, actual cost, a manual invoice status, an invoice reference, and margin notes. It does not generate invoices, accept payments, connect to accounting systems, or reconcile revenue.
