Before you get started

Relay pricing questions.

Know the product boundaries and agree on the terms before purchase.

How is Relay priced?

Relay is priced by quote. Ask us to confirm the subscription price, billing period, included usage, and cancellation terms in writing before you decide.

Are there feature tiers or paid add-ons?

The workspace plan includes all core workflows. Any limits or additional charges must be stated in the quote before purchase.

Does Relay connect to external project tools?

Relay is a standalone workspace and does not synchronize with or configure external project tools.

What does Relay help track?

Missing or invalid customer responses, unassigned tasks, explicit blockers, overdue tasks, and work waiting on prerequisites.
