Good to know

Pricing and plan questions.

Clear boundaries before you choose a plan.

Are taxes included?

No. Starter and Growth prices are before applicable taxes. Relay confirms applicable taxes and the total before payment.

What counts as an active implementation?

An implementation is active while it has unfinished tasks or required customer information that has not been submitted. Completed implementations do not use an active slot.

What can customers see in the portal?

Customers see only tasks explicitly shared with them and information intended for their portal. Internal rates, budgets, and margin metrics remain in the Relay workspace.

What files can customers upload?

Only scoped CSV/XLSX uploads for tasks configured with a validation schema are supported. Malware scanning must be configured before uploads can be used; general PDF and image attachments are not supported.

Do integrations and email reminders work automatically?

No. A workspace admin must configure Slack alerts. HubSpot and email reminders require provider setup and owner configuration; email reminders also require scheduled processing. You can use Relay without enabling these optional services.

Does Relay generate invoices or process payments?

No. Custom includes financial controls and manual invoice-status/reference tracking. Relay does not generate invoices, accept payments, connect to accounting systems, or reconcile revenue.
